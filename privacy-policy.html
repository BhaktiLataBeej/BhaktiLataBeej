Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 15, 2026

Welcome to Bhakti Lata Beej ("we", "us", "our"). We are committed to respecting your privacy and providing a safe and meaningful spiritual and educational experience for children, teenagers, adults, families, and communities.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

Our website is designed to be accessible without requiring you to create an account or submit personal information.

At present, we do not intentionally collect personal information through the website, including:

  • Names
  • Phone numbers
  • Email addresses submitted through forms
  • Postal addresses
  • Dates of birth
  • Photographs or videos uploaded by visitors
  • User accounts or passwords
  • Payment information
  • Personal profiles

We currently do not provide registration, login, contact forms, newsletter subscriptions, commenting, or user-upload functionality on this website.

2. Information Automatically Processed

When you visit any website, certain technical information may be processed by the website's hosting or infrastructure providers in order to deliver, maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the website.

This may include information such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser and device information
  • Operating system
  • Date and time of access
  • Technical request information

We do not intentionally use this information to create personal profiles or track individuals across websites.

3. Third-Party Services and Links

Our website contains links to external services and platforms, including:

  • YouTube
  • Instagram
  • WhatsApp
  • Email services
  • Other websites or services that may be referenced from time to time

These links are provided so that visitors can learn more about Bhakti Lata Beej, communicate with us, or participate in our wider community.

When you click one of these links, you may leave our website and interact directly with the relevant third-party service. Those services operate independently and may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

We do not control the privacy practices of third-party platforms.

4. Children and Young People

Bhakti Lata Beej provides spiritual and educational content intended for a broad audience that may include children, teenagers, adults, families, and educators.

We do not intentionally request or collect personal information from children through this website.

We encourage parents and guardians to guide younger users when they interact with external platforms such as YouTube, Instagram, WhatsApp, or other third-party services.

We do not ask children to provide personal information such as their home address, telephone number, school information, photographs, or other personal details through this website.

If our website or services are changed in the future to collect personal information from children, we will review and update our practices and policies as required by applicable law.

5. Cookies and Tracking

At present, we do not intentionally use cookies or tracking technologies on this website for advertising, behavioural profiling, or analytics.

Some technical cookies or similar technologies may be used by hosting infrastructure or third-party services where necessary for their operation. Such technologies are controlled by the relevant service provider.

If we introduce analytics, advertising, login systems, personalized services, or other technologies that materially change how personal information is processed, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

6. How We Use Information

Because we do not currently operate a user registration or data-collection system, we do not maintain a database of visitor profiles.

Any technical information processed as part of hosting and securing the website is used for legitimate operational purposes such as:

  • Delivering website content
  • Maintaining website functionality
  • Preventing abuse or security incidents
  • Diagnosing technical problems
  • Maintaining the reliability and security of the website

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade visitors' personal information.

We do not intentionally share personal information collected through this website with advertisers or data brokers.

Third-party infrastructure and service providers may process technical information as necessary to provide their services.

8. External Websites

Our website may link to websites and services operated by third parties.

Once you leave our website, the privacy practices of the third-party service apply. We encourage visitors and parents or guardians to review the privacy policies and terms of those services before providing personal information or using their features.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of our website to maintain its security and prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or misuse.

However, no internet transmission or website can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, technology, or legal obligations change.

The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
