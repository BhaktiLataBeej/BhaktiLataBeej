PRIVACY POLICY

Application: DivineQuest
Published by: Bhakti Lata Beej
Support Email: supportbhaktilatabeej@gmail.com
Public Webpage URL: https://bhaktilatabeej.com/divinequest/privacy-policy

1. INTRODUCTION

Welcome to DivineQuest, an educational and spiritual quiz application published by Bhakti Lata Beej.

DivineQuest is designed to help children and families explore spiritual knowledge, stories and values through interactive quizzes based on our Vedic traditions.

We respect the privacy of our users, particularly children. DivineQuest has been designed as an offline application with a privacy-first approach.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is used by the application, how it is stored and how users can manage it.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

DivineQuest does not collect or transmit personal information to Bhakti Lata Beej or any external server.

The application may use the following information locally on the user's device:

  • User name or nickname: Entered by the user to personalize the application experience.
  • Game progress: Quiz scores, completed activities and other gameplay progress, where applicable.

This information is stored locally on the user's device and is used only to provide the application's features.

We do not operate user accounts or maintain a central database of user information.

We encourage children to use a nickname rather than their full legal name.

3. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

The information stored locally is used only to:

  • Personalize the application experience.
  • Maintain quiz scores and progress.
  • Remember gameplay information between sessions.
  • Improve the user's learning experience within the application.

This information is not used for advertising, profiling or tracking.

4. DATA SHARING AND THIRD PARTIES

DivineQuest does not sell, rent or share users' personal information with third parties.

The application does not use advertising services, third-party analytics or tracking services, subject to the technical configuration of the published version.

No user name or gameplay information is transmitted to external servers by DivineQuest.

5. INTERNET CONNECTIVITY

DivineQuest is designed to operate completely offline.

An internet connection is not required to access or use its core features.

The application does not require users to register, log in or provide an email address or phone number.

6. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

DivineQuest is designed with children and families in mind.

We follow a privacy-first approach and seek to minimise the information used by the application.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through external servers.

We encourage parents and guardians to guide children in using appropriate nicknames and avoiding the entry of sensitive personal information.

Parents or guardians may contact us using the email address provided below if they have any questions or concerns about the application's privacy practices.

7. DATA STORAGE, RETENTION AND DELETION

The user's name or nickname and gameplay progress are stored locally on the device.

Bhakti Lata Beej does not maintain a copy of this information on its servers.

Users or their parents/guardians can remove locally stored application information by clearing the application's storage/data through Android device settings or by uninstalling the application.

Please note that clearing application data or uninstalling the app may permanently remove saved gameplay progress.

Device-level backup settings, if enabled, may affect how locally stored application data is backed up by the device operating system.

8. DATA SECURITY

Since DivineQuest is designed to store gameplay information locally and does not transmit it to our servers, we minimise the risks associated with external data transmission.
